## Claim and proof structure

The source asserts unrestricted Koebe uniformization: every sphere domain has a conformal circle-domain model. It additionally proves a retained-test transfer/selection theorem with exact agreement on exhausting cores, vanishing added Dirichlet energy, exact periods, and vanishing counting-l2 positive excess over existing boundary-band budgets. The hyperbolic corollary is restricted to complete genus-zero metrics of curvature exactly -1 and imports Luo–Wu; it does not assert uniqueness or the general curvature-at-least-minus-one variant.

The proof separates two limit obstructions. Unmarked complementary components must collapse to points; marked components must equal their followed limiting round disk rather than contain a protrusion. The quotient obtained by collapsing original complementary components is a sphere with compact totally disconnected dust. Smooth compatible coordinates on the original domain carry finite energy and l2 boundary oscillation budgets. Finite transfer freezes a core, changes the conformal structure outside it, and uses shifted staircases to make all but one coordinate constant on filling collars. The fillings have differential rank at most one. A common metric built from the sum of derivative tensors converts small exterior-product integrals into small summed energy; normalized finite circle-domain uniformization then supplies limiting maps and component labels.

The barrier machinery uses tagged paths and repeated-visit boundary tolls, with explicit residual-incidence bounds against earlier laws/levels. Actual collar splicing replaces compactness arguments while retaining constraints, and shrinking-jump removal preserves positive cost. A clipped chain distance gives a barrier with controlled Sobolev norm and pointwise bands; shrinking positive smoothing preserves every dust band and free trace. If small barriers are unavailable, pointwise modulus duality gives laws with L2 mean occupation, prescribed-countable l2 expected visits, and absolutely continuous tag tuples. A separate fusion theorem turns uniform countable/atomless tests into one whole-dust l2 hit law.

Diffuse dust meetings are removed using the residual constraints and compatibility. Four paths with alternating collar starts force a planar meeting, whereas localized occupation and hit tails make it unlikely. Therefore only countably many ends can lack small compatible barriers. A persistent maximal countable selection and the parallel-line payment inequality collapse all other components.

At a remaining marked end, average integer indices of compactly supported lifted simple-arc laws. The resulting period-one coordinate has small energy and nonsingular l2 bands, and its boundary derivative is a positive probability measure. Gates near a protrusion give a fixed positive lower bound for a boundary integral; Stokes gives a smaller upper bound from energy and nonsingular bands. One fixed test excludes a whole neighborhood of a bad pair `(f,D_b)` and keeps doing so after all later compatible extensions. Second countability permits countably many such tests, yielding equality of each marked component and its round disk. The main theorem follows with an empty seed list; the scalar retained-test corollary uses pairwise area-null dust images to verify compatibility.
