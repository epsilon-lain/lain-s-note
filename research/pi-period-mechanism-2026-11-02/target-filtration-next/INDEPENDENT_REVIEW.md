# Independent review of mixed time and normal-ideal cuts

2026-11-02. Reviewed `MIXED_NORMAL_IDEAL_CUTS.md` and `verify_mixed_normal_cuts.py`, and reran the full verifier. The ideal-annihilation bound, exact ambient lattice count, selected-space inequality, equal-weight asymptotic, and finite phase example are valid under the stated hypotheses. No mathematical correction was needed.

1. Under the displayed period substitution, every generator X_i-X_1 becomes u_i-u_1 and contains no time variable. The factor (Y-1)^k contributes order at least k in z. A product of r generators from the chosen subset has total degree r in the u coordinates indexed by {1} union S. Coefficient extraction therefore kills the mixed ideal whenever either t<k or a_1+sum_(i in S) a_i<r. Multiplication by the remaining polynomial cannot remove either minimum order. This argument applies to arbitrary target labels and every formal or complex Z; no lower-set closure of those labels is assumed.

2. Restricting the column map to W intersect J_(k,r) gives image dimension at most the number R_(k,r) of potentially surviving rows. Adding a complementary column space of dimension M-d_(k,r) proves the finite rank bound. Potential survival need not imply nonzero entries, which only makes the bound weaker. The count is therefore safe for arbitrary selected polynomial columns.

3. The cheapest-anchor requirement w_1<=w_i is exactly the condition needed for both X_i=U_i+T and U_i=X_i-X_1 to preserve the weighted column space. Translation Y=y+1 has the same property. In the adapted coordinates the ideal is monomial, so the ambient ideal dimension is precisely the claimed lattice count, not merely a lower bound. No norm or denominator estimate is carried through this coordinate transformation.

4. For W contained in V_H, the subspace-intersection inequality d_(k,r)(W)>=M-N_H+D_(k,r) is valid even when its right side is negative. Substituting it in the selected-space rank bound yields N_H-D_(k,r)+R_(k,r). Taking the minimum with the number of rows gives the asserted ambient-column bound. For fixed raw columns, the rank of their coefficient map to the quotient is exactly M-d_(k,r)(W), as used by the verifier.

5. In equal spatial weights, the total budget in the m-1 normal coordinates has Beta(m-1,3) distribution under simplex volume: time, tangent, and unused budget contribute the remaining three barycentric coordinates. Integrating x^(m-2)(1-x)^2 produces the displayed polynomial CDF. An O(1) phase perturbation of the normal-degree cutoff is negligible in the fixed-parameter H limit. The subsequent high-dimensional decay at fixed A<1 is correct. The note explicitly restricts this conclusion to equal weights; no separated-weight rank theorem is inferred.

6. The large phase example satisfies the stated strict time-budget hypothesis and all finite time cuts. The verifier passed 144 exact small selected-ideal/rank inequalities, 84 time cuts, 45 positive Pascal blocks, and 4,420 mixed ideal bounds on the complete 28,000-row packet. Independent direct summation confirmed 16,548 ambient adapted columns of normal degree below 9, exactly 958 target rows of total transverse order at least 9, and 9,972 ambient columns visible to the source. Thus target rank is at most 17,506, while the source is also rank deficient. This last limitation is essential and is correctly stated: the example does not refute a method that assumes an actually nonzero source minor.

The general theorem is a rank upper bound from ideals. The finite example supplies a genuine obstruction to relying on time support and nonzero transport alone. Neither supplies an actual-period determinant magnitude estimate, a counterexample in the original strongly separated parameter path, or a conclusion about pi's bad approximability.
